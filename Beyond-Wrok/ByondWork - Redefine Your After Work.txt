ByondWork - Redefine Your After Work Hours

Problem
In today’s fast-paced corporate world, especially in metro cities, professionals often:
Struggle to productively spend their after-work hours.
Fall into unproductive habits (OTT, social media), leading to physical inactivity and social isolation.
Lack access to people or infrastructure to engage in sports, wellness, or recreational activities.

 Solution — ByondWork
A community-based sports and wellness platform exclusively designed for working professionals to:
Discover and book sports & recreational activities.
Create or join events like cricket matches, pickleball games, or wellness workshops.
Network through play — connect with like-minded professionals on and off the field.

Key Features
LinkedIn Integration for seamless onboarding and professional networking (Subject to research).
Facility & Event Booking (individual or group) with transparent cost splitting in the platform.
Community Groups for building local interest-based networks.
Leaderboards & Player Profiles(Corporate and GLobal Leaderboards) to track performance and promote friendly competition.
Training and Coaching: Book training courses including swimming, strength training, pickleball etc.
Wellness Workshops:  Book wellness workshops like zumba classes,yoga class, recreational workshops etc.
Corporate Events for companies to manage and organize internal employee events.

Target Audience
Corporate professionals (especially 22–40 yrs) in metro cities.
HR departments and corporate wellness managers.
Young working population living away from home.

 Why Now?
Growing focus on work-life balance and employee wellness.
Rise of recreational sports culture among urban professionals.
Increasing demand for community-driven offline engagement in a post-remote work era.

Revenue Model
Commission on facility bookings and event fees.
Premium memberships (exclusive events, leaderboards, analytics).
B2B partnerships with companies for employee wellness programs.
Sponsored events and brand tie-ups in sports & wellness space.

 Vision
To become India’s leading after-work engagement platform, blending health, community, and professional networking — one game at a time.
